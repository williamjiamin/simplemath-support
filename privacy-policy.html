SimpleMath Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Overview

SimpleMath is a bilingual elementary-math learning app. WANG LIPAI designed it so that learners can use its core learning features without creating an account or providing personal information to us.

Information and learning progress

SimpleMath stores an optional learner display name, language and theme choices, and learning progress on the device only. This information is used solely to provide the app's features. It is not transmitted to WANG LIPAI, advertisers, analytics providers, or other third parties.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, share, or track personal data.
  • We do not use advertising, third-party analytics, accounts, or in-app purchases.
  • We do not make network requests as part of the app's learning experience.

Children's privacy

SimpleMath is designed for young learners. Because learning data remains on the device and is not sent to us, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can remove the optional display name or change settings from within the app. Deleting SimpleMath from the device deletes the learning data stored by the app on that device, subject to the device's normal backup settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date before the change takes effect.
