SimpleMath — Kids Math

SimpleMath is an age-appropriate, bilingual elementary-math learning app for children ages 6–8. It combines visual lessons, short practice rounds, gentle review, daily challenges, and collectible rewards.

The app has no ads, accounts, analytics, or tracking. Optional Premium features are available only after an in-app parent gate and are processed by Apple.
